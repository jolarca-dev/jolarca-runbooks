# ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.29 — ICT Readiness for Business Continuity

## Control intent

A.5.29 requires that ICT continuity be **planned, implemented, maintained, and tested** so that
information and communication technology keeps supporting the business during a disruption. The
control is about *readiness*: documented plans, defined recovery objectives, and proof the plans
work — not the plans alone.
